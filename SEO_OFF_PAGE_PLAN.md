3. **Verified Local Footprint:** Flawless Name, Address, Phone (NAP) consistency across verified business profiles in New Delhi.
4. **Artisan Storytelling & Digital PR:** Coverage of traditional hand-goldsmithing methods, family heritage dating to 1950, and ethical bespoke creation.

## 4. Educational Resource & Gemological Citation Strategy

When third-party writers, lifestyle bloggers, and astrological educators research gemstone weight calculations, they frequently encounter confusion between Ratti and Carats.

By publishing the authoritative, mathematically verified guide to **Pakka Ratti (182 mg / 0.91 ct) vs Sunari Ratti (121.5 mg / ~0.61 ct)** on `/gemstone-guide.html`, Gulshan Jewellers becomes the primary citeable source for this metrology question across the Indian web.

### Natural Link Attraction Strategy:
1. Provide copy-paste citation code snippets for journalists and gemologists.
2. Publish high-resolution downloadable conversion charts with Gulshan Jewellers watermark.
3. Monitor queries on platforms like Reddit (`r/jewelry`, `r/gemstones`, `r/IndiaThriftStore`) and Quora, answering technical grading queries with objective, educational references.
