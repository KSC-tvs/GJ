Ethos of Transparency

Know What You Wear

Don't only ask how many carats. Ask whether it is natural, whether it has been treated, and whether independent certification is available.

◈

Natural vs Lab-Grown

We clearly distinguish earth-mined gems from synthetic lab stones. Every natural stone was formed over millions of years inside the earth.

◆

Treatment Disclosure

Thermal heating, oiling, and glass-fillings dramatically alter value. We disclose all treatment statuses transparently before you decide.

☉

Independent Certification

Every significant gemstone is paired with documentation from accredited testing laboratories (e.g. IIGJ or equivalent reputable labs).

✦

Hallmarked Purity

22K, 18K, 14K gold pieces are BIS hallmarked with verified karat stamps. Sterling silver is guaranteed solid 925 alloy.
