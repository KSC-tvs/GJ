The Artisan Process

Four Steps to an Heirloom

From first conversation to finished jewel, transparent at every stage.

01

Share Your Vision

Share your idea, occasion, preferred metal, gemstone interest, or budget parameters. No commission is too grand or too subtle.

02

Stone & Setting Direction

Examine certified loose earth-mined gems. Discuss pavilion depth, light entry, claw vs bezel mountings, and comfortable ergonomics.

03

Approve Bespoke Design

Review detailed wax renderings, setting proportions, and complete metal weight and hallmarking specifications prior to casting.

04

Receive Your Heirloom

Hand-forged by our senior karigars, hand-burnished, stamped with government hallmarks, and presented with certificate documentation.
