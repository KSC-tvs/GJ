How to Evaluate a Fine Gemstone

Fine gemstones are among the earth’s rarest treasures. We believe genuine appreciation begins with knowledge: understanding treatments, origins, clarity, and the honest differences between commercial hype and genuine rarity.

“Don’t only ask how many carats. Ask whether it is natural, whether it has been treated, and whether independent certification is available.”

— Gulshan Jewellers Guiding Principle
The 6 Foundations

The Evaluation Framework

Six objective criteria every buyer should verify before investing in a gemstone.

01

Natural vs Laboratory-Grown

Lab-grown gems simulate physical and chemical crystal structures in weeks. Earth-mined gems formed over millions of years. For astrological harmony and enduring legacy, only natural stones possess authentic geological origin.

02

Treatment Disclosure

Over 95% of commercial sapphires and rubies are heat-treated or diffusion-colored. Completely untreated stones are exceptionally scarce. Always insist on knowing if heating, beryllium diffusion, or glass-filling took place.

03

Color & Saturation

True quality is determined by hue, tone, and saturation. A fine gem should not be excessively dark or pale. It should glow with vivid internal fire under natural morning daylight.

04

Clarity & Inclusions

In natural stones, inclusions are earth fingerprints, not defects. Microscopic rutile silk proves untreated corundum. Beware of 'glassy clean' cheap stones, which are often synthetic or heavily resin-filled.

05

Origin When Verified

Ceylon, Kashmir, Colombia, and Burma carry historical lore. However, origin should only be claimed when accompanied by advanced spectroscopy from an accredited gemological laboratory.

06

Independent Certification

Never rely exclusively on a seller's oral word. An independent laboratory report (e.g. IIGJ or equivalent testing authority) provides scientific proof of species, origin, and treatment status.

Pukhraj · Brihaspati

Yellow Sapphire (Pukhraj)

Revered as the gemstone of Jupiter (Brihaspati), Yellow Sapphire is associated with wisdom, prosperity, and auspicious life beginnings. Our atelier specializes in untreated Ceylon (Sri Lanka) specimens with vivid canary and golden-honey saturation.

Evaluation Highlights for Pukhraj

  • • Origin: Ceylon (Sri Lanka) is the benchmark for vibrant untreated saturation.
  • • Treatment: Demand an explicit unheated certificate. Reject beryllium diffusion.
  • • Metal Setting: 22K yellow gold or Panchdhatu with an open pavilion basket for natural skin contact.
  • • Carat Weights: Typically 3.50 ct to 6.00 ct (4 to 7 Ratti) based on personal consultation.

Neelam · Shani

Blue Sapphire (Neelam)

Neelam is among the most intensely scrutinized gems in the world. Associated with Saturn (Shani), it commands disciplined evaluation. We curate deep royal blue corundum free from dark extinction, mounted in platinum, 18K white gold, or sterling silver.

Evaluation Highlights for Neelam

  • • Color Balance: Cornflower to deep royal blue without harsh zoning or inky opacity.
  • • Thermal Status: Disclose natural unheated vs minimally heated status per piece.
  • • Personal Comfort: Traditional practice recommends close personal inspection during consultation.

Panna · Budha

Emerald (Panna)

The gem of Mercury (Budha), celebrated for intellect, communication, and balanced vitality. Emeralds inherently possess a natural 'jardin'—an organic network of inclusions that verifies earth crystallization.

Evaluation Highlights for Emerald

  • • Jardin: Inclusions verify earth origin; clarity is assessed by luminosity and vibrant color.
  • • Enhancement: Traditional colorless cedarwood oil is disclosed; resin and dye are strictly avoided.
  • • Setting: Protective bezels or corner prongs in 18K yellow gold prevent facet edge abrasion.

Manik · Surya

Natural Ruby (Manik)

Known as ‘Ratnaraj’—King of Precious Stones. The gemstone of the Sun (Surya), natural ruby embodies vitality, royal legacy, and self-possession. We offer unheated Mozambique and Burma specimens with rich crimson saturation.

Evaluation Highlights for Ruby

  • • Lead-Glass Warning: Reject commercially flooded lead-glass composites; insist on genuine earth-mined corundum.
  • • Fluorescence: Authentic rubies emit a warm crimson luminescence in sunlight.
  • • Metal Harmony: Forged in 18K or 22K yellow gold to complement warm crimson fire.
