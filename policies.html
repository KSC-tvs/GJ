1. Insured Shipping & Handover Protocol

Complimentary Transit Insurance Across India: Every parcel dispatched by Gulshan Jewellers is fully insured for its declared value from the moment it departs our atelier until physical handover to you.

Discreet, Tamper-Evident Packaging: Packages bear no external jewellery branding to guarantee transit security. Each piece is nestled within a solid velvet box, enveloped in a serialized tamper-evident security seal.

Dispatch Timelines:

3. Hallmarking Standards & Gemological Certification

Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Hallmarked Gold: All gold creations carry official government BIS hallmark laser engravings verifying purity:

  • 22K Gold (916 Purity): 91.6% pure gold — traditional jewellery standard.
  • 18K Gold (750 Purity): 75.0% pure gold — European and modern fine jewellery standard.
  • 14K Gold (585 Purity): 58.5% pure gold — resilient everyday fine diamond wear.
  • 925 Sterling Silver: Guaranteed minimum 92.5% pure silver with hand-stamped hallmark.

Independent Gemological Certification: Every natural precious gemstone (Pukhraj, Neelam, Emerald, Ruby) is accompanied by independent accredited gemological laboratory documentation (e.g. IIGJ or equivalent recognized testing facilities) disclosing mineral species, natural earth-mined origin, and thermal treatment status.

Strict Treatment Transparency: We never sell lead-glass filled rubies, beryllium-diffused sapphires, or resin-injected emeralds. All standard traditional enhancements (such as minor cedarwood oil in emeralds) are disclosed in writing.

4. Jewellery Care & Maintenance Guide

Diamonds & Corundum (Sapphires & Rubies): Soak for 5 minutes in lukewarm water with mild liquid soap. Gently clean the underside with a soft-bristled toothbrush and rinse thoroughly with clear water.

Emeralds (Beryl): Emeralds require gentle care. Never use ultrasonic cleaners or hot water. Wipe gently with a soft microfiber cloth dampened with room-temperature water. Keep away from perfumes and cosmetics.

Sterling Silver: Store clean pieces in dry, airtight pouches to minimize natural atmospheric oxidation. Use a dry silver polishing cloth to buff highlights. Avoid liquid silver dips on hand-oxidized artisan pieces.
